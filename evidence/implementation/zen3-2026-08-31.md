## Implemented changes

1. Sender race: a `SenderSession` owns one thread and one socket. Cleanup closes only
   that session's local socket. Stop detaches the session under the lock, then performs
   stop request and join outside the lock; restart starts a replacement only after the
   old thread has exited. This gives at most one sender and one intended socket and is
   safe for repeated start, watchdog recovery, and `onDestroy`.
2. Watchdog: fix age is calculated from elapsed nanoseconds correctly. Recovery
   backoff resets only after 30 successful UDP sends or 120 seconds after recovery,
   and logs the selected reset reason. A recovery starts a fresh success streak.
3. Notifications: patrol now verifies selected marker/source, `#* ZEN3`, positive
   reach, and chrony `Leap status : Normal` for Zen3 recovery. Fallback requires an
   actual selected `^*` Internet source and synchronised chrony. Controller transitions
   that do not match chrony are recorded as `notification_mismatch` and do not notify;
   a verified pending transition is emitted once.
4. chronyd restart: controller reasserts its fixed selection profile every 30 seconds
   while running, including when its state value did not change. The unit also has
   `BindsTo` and `PartOf` for `chrony.service` without broadening the existing wrapper
   authority. The live abnormal-exit/single-start path remains unverified offline.
